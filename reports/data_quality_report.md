## 5. Residual items and impact

- **Price-outlier rows** kept but flagged: they hold 23,842.40 of 3,738,533.15 total sales (0.6%) and 23,333.16 of 700,783.13 total profit (3.3%). Power BI and SQL expose the flag so the analyst can show KPIs with and without them.

- **Ship date missing** on 14 rows (invalid dates blanked, flagged `ship_date_invalid`).

- **Postal code 'Unknown'** on 39 rows.

- **Quarantined rows** are in `data/processed/rejected_rows.csv` with the reason for each.

- **Limitation:** the rules above detect what they were written to detect. Plausible-looking but wrong values (e.g. a wrong but valid product price within 5x of normal) cannot be found without a source system to compare against.
